Leo Lu

The Explorer Behind The Camera

Personal Introduction

I’m Leo Lu, a student at Shanghai Maple Leaf International School. At home, my family jokes that I’m “the one always behind the camera.” Whenever we travel or have family events, I’m usually the person taking photos, trying to capture small, real moments. Sometimes I spend too long editing a single photo, but I enjoy the process.

At school, my friends often see me as a reliable lab partner. When experiments fail, which happens more often than I’d like, I stay calm and go through the steps again to figure out what went wrong.

One experience I’m proud of is last year’s science fair. I led a small team to build a wind tunnel. We had many problems with the sensors, and it was frustrating at times. I read manuals, learned basic soldering, and kept testing until it finally worked. In the end, we won first place. That experience showed me that solving real problems is much more meaningful than just following instructions. After high school, I plan to study Engineering or Applied Science at UBC.

Research Projects & Awards

I was the team leader for our wind tunnel project. I planned the research and assigned tasks such as building models and collecting data. When our results were inconsistent, I suggested using ABS plastic and adjustable stands, which improved stability and gave us better data.

High School Reflection

Looking back, I have learned the most from challenges. A crashed drone, failed sensors, and team disagreements were all difficult at the time, but they helped me grow. I learned patience, logical thinking, and how to listen to others.

I also realized that good teamwork matters. The best results came when we discussed problems together instead of working separately. Both photography and experiments taught me to pay attention to details. I have changed a lot since Grade 9, and I am ready for new challenges in university.
