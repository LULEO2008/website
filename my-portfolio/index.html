About Me

My family calls me "an explorer behind the camera". I love capturing small details and happy moments in life using photography. I always take pictures during family trips to save our memories. Learning photo editing helps me make these pictures even better.

At school, my friends see me as a "reliable lab partner". When our group experiments fail, I stay calm and figure out the problem step by step.

Winning first prize at the school science fair is my proudest moment. I led a team to build a small wind tunnel. Our sensors broke many times during the project. I fixed them by reading manuals and learning how to solder wires. That experience showed me how important it is to never give up and to keep exploring.

I also realized that good teamwork is better than doing everything alone. Both my camera and my science experiments proved that small details create big progress. I am very proud of my improvements during high school. Now, I feel ready for exciting new challenges at university.
